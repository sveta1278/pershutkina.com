Personalised offer experiments

A/B tests of personalised offers

Task. The team needed to know which personalised offers, and which placements, are profitable for which user cohorts.

What I did

  • Initiated a cohort-based testing framework for personalised offers and their placement.
  • Designed and analysed 10 A/B tests: sample sizes, significance, effects by cohort.
