Whoosh · 2025 – 2026

Personalised offer experiments

10
A/B tests of personalised offers

Task. The team needed to know which personalised offers, and which placements, are profitable for which user cohorts.

What I did

  • Initiated a cohort-based testing framework for personalised offers and their placement.
  • Designed and analysed 10 A/B tests: sample sizes, significance, effects by cohort.
  • Flagged the two tests whose samples were too small to draw a conclusion.

Result. Eight tests gave significant results; I reported the two underpowered ones as inconclusive rather than over-reading them. The significant results became a margin-ranked offer matrix by cohort that now guides offer selection.

Stack: SQL, Python, A/B testing, cohort analysis

Relevant for: Senior Data Analyst, Senior Product Analyst, Analytics Lead, Data Product Manager

Harvard Spatial Data Lab · 2026 – now

CompactLife: comparing homes by daily routine

Task. Home search usually compares commute time only, while a real day also includes shopping, care and other stops.

What I did

  • Defined the first product version: user-entered stops, feasible multimodal routes, opening hours, arrival deadlines and walking preferences.
  • Designed the data architecture: raw, clean and model-ready layers over ODPT transit feeds and OpenStreetMap, with source timestamps, dataset versions and validation checks.
  • Planned delivery in stages that separate a useful MVP from later machine-learning features.

Result. In development for the Open Data Challenge for Public Transportation 2026; not deployed yet.

Stack: PostGIS, OpenTripPlanner, OpenStreetMap, ODPT open data, Python
